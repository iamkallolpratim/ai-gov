### Is this legal advice?

No. The console helps you track and document compliance work. Classifications and findings
come from the metadata you supply and the rules configured here. Every finding cites its
source so a qualified person can verify it before you rely on it.

### Which regulations are covered?

Detection and risk classification cover the EU AI Act, California ADMT, China's generative
AI measures and India's DPDP Act. Rule-by-rule policy evaluation is deepest for the EU AI
Act. Starter packs for the other three are on the v0.1 roadmap. See the
[regulation pages](../regulations/eu-ai-act/).

### Why does a system deployed only in the US show China as applicable?

China's measures reach services that are *accessible* from China or whose output is
viewable there. If `service_accessible_regions` or `content_accessible_regions` includes
`GLOBAL`, China applies. Narrow those fields if access is genuinely restricted.

### Does it send data anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your infrastructure, and there is no telemetry.

### Can I add my own rules or jurisdictions?

Yes. Rules are Rego files with tests, and a jurisdiction is data plus tests. See
[Writing Rego policies](../policies/writing-rego/) and
[Contributing](../contributing/).

### What happens if OPA is down?

The check falls back to the policy's declarative rules and records `engine: "rules"`. If
there are none, the check is stored as `error`, never silently dropped.

### Can I run it without authentication?

Only on a private network you control. See
[authentication modes](../self-hosting/#authentication-modes).
