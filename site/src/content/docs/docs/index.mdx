The AI Governance Console is an open-source, self-hosted system that treats AI compliance as
software. It keeps an **inventory** of your AI systems, works out **which jurisdictions
apply and why**, **classifies risk** under each one, **evaluates** every system against
machine-readable policies in Open Policy Agent, and produces **audit-ready evidence
packages**. An append-only trail sits behind every decision.

It models four regimes today: the **EU AI Act**, **California's ADMT rules**, **China's
generative AI measures** and **India's DPDP Act**, plus an organisation-wide baseline.

:::caution[Not legal advice]
The console helps you *track and document* compliance work. It does not certify compliance
with any regulation. Every finding cites its source so a qualified person can verify it.
:::
