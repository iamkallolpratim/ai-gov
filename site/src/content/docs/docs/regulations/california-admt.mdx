## Applicability

Deployment or users in California, residents as data subjects, data residency, or
automated consequential decisions about California residents in employment, credit,
housing, insurance, education or essential services. Children's data amplifies.

## Risk tiers and obligations

| Tier | Label | Obligations attached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high | Significant-decision ADMT | pre-use notice to consumers; opt-out and human appeal route; annual risk assessment filed with the CPPA |
| limited | Disclosure-required AI | disclose that content or interaction is AI-generated |
| minimal | Minimal risk | none |

High tier: employment screening, credit scoring, essential services, education assessment.
Systems that make automated decisions are raised to at least *limited*.
