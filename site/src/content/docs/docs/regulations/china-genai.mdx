## Applicability

China's reach is the broadest the console models. Beyond deployment, offering, data
subjects and data residency, a system is in scope when the **service is reachable from
China** or its **generated content is viewable in China**. Generative AI and biometric
processing amplify.

## Risk tiers and obligations

| Tier | Label | Obligations attached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prohibited | Prohibited service | untargeted face scraping, social scoring |
| high | Service with public-opinion attributes | algorithm filing with the CAC; security assessment before public launch; labelling of synthetic media; data localisation for personal information |
| limited | Filing-only service | algorithm filing; content labelling |
| minimal | Minimal risk | none |

Every in-scope system is at least *limited*. Generative AI escalates to *high*.
