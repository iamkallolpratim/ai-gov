## Applicability

Deployment, offering, data subjects or data residency anywhere in the EU/EEA, or
automated consequential decisions about EU residents. Biometric processing, children's data
and generative AI act as amplifiers.

## Risk tiers

| Tier | Label | Examples from the taxonomy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prohibited | Prohibited practice (Art. 5) | social scoring, subliminal manipulation, workplace emotion recognition, untargeted face scraping, individual predictive policing |
| high | High-risk (Annex III / Art. 6) | employment screening, credit scoring, biometric identification, law enforcement, critical infrastructure, education assessment, migration, essential services, medical diagnosis; any safety component |
| limited | Limited risk, transparency (Art. 50) | chatbots, synthetic content |
| minimal | Minimal risk | everything else |

## What the policies check

### `aigov.eu.high_risk` (Chapter III Section 2)

In scope for Annex III use cases, systems classified high or prohibited, and safety
components. A documented Art. 6(3) derogation removes the system from scope.

| Article | Check | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art. 9 | risk management system exists | critical |
| Art. 10 | training, validation and test data documented | high |
| Art. 10(5) | safeguards where special-category data is processed | critical |
| Art. 11 | Annex IV technical documentation recorded | critical |
| Art. 12 | automatic logging enabled | high |
| Art. 13 | instructions for use published | medium |
| Art. 14 | human oversight documented, effective for autonomous systems, individual review route | critical / high |
| Art. 15 | accuracy metrics declared; adversarial robustness tested for biometrics | high |
| Art. 25 | written agreement with upstream model providers | medium |
| Art. 27 | fundamental rights impact assessment where required | high |
| Art. 43 | conformity assessment completed | critical |
| Art. 49 | Annex III system registered in the EU database | high |
| Art. 72 / 73 | post-market monitoring plan; serious-incident reporting | medium / high |

Annex III use cases are mapped by point, so an employment screener is told it falls under
"Annex III(4), recruitment and selection of natural persons" rather than a generic "high-risk".

### `aigov.eu.prohibited` (Art. 5)

Flags prohibited practices from the use case and metadata signals.

### `aigov.eu.transparency` (Art. 50)

Disclosure duties for systems that interact with people or generate synthetic content,
plus the downstream side of Art. 53: a system built on general-purpose models must have the
provider's technical documentation and training-content summary on record.

## Not yet modelled

The GPAI provider obligations themselves (Art. 53–55, including systemic-risk models) and
a full Annex III walk-through are planned for v0.2.
